# Kudzu app icon brief

## What Kudzu is
Kudzu is a quiet, native macOS text expander. You type a short trigger like `:sig` and it grows into a whole passage. The product's personality is a calm notebook: neutral paper, one living chartreuse, and nothing that shouts.

## The icon
A single computer keycap that fills the entire icon.

The legend printed on the key is a **sprouting colon**. The colon is the character people type before a trigger.
- The bottom mark is a plain round dot.
- The top mark is a dot that has just begun turning into a leaf. One side of the circle draws out into a soft, pointed leaf tip angled up and to the right, with a thin vein running through it.

It should read as a keycap first, a colon second, and only then as a leaf, so the wit lands on a second look. That is the whole idea: a keystroke that grows. One object, one idea, nothing else.

## How the keycap should feel
- **A real, premium key.** Picture a well-made keyboard key you'd want to press: the wide, sculpted top surface of a low-profile Mac keycap, very slightly dished.
- **Its sides fill the canvas.** You're looking at it from just above the front. The skirt shows on the left, right and bottom; the top face sits high, with only a sliver of edge above it.
- **The material:** matte, warm, soft-touch plastic in paper white. It's like good paper or unglazed porcelain, not glossy plastic or chrome. Light falls softly from above, so the top face is the brightest surface and the sides fall gently into shade.
- **The legend** looks printed or inlaid into the key face: crisp, flush, part of the key. It doesn't float on a sticker. The chartreuse leaf has a faint inner glow of colour, as if lit slightly from within. The olive dot is solid and grounded.
- **Overall:** tactile, quiet, confident and precise. Something you could hold, made with restraint. Think Apple hardware photography, not an illustration.

Kudzu's palette. The icon uses the paper neutrals for the key, `vine` for the leaf and the deep olive for the dot. **Chartreuse appears only on the leaf**: it's the one accent, so nothing else may be chartreuse.

## Avoid
- extra elements: no text, letters, vines, stems, flowers, sparkles or a cursor
- a tilted or floating key, or dramatic perspective
- glossy, chrome, inflated or toy-like plastic
- glows, gradients or texture behind the key
- outlines or strokes on the shapes
- anything that looks like generic "AI art": over-detailed, busy or glossy for its own sake
