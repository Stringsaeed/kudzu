# Keycap material source

The keycap is a full-canvas, warm paper-white, low-profile keyboard key. It has a softly dished face, fine matte texture, a rounded lip, and gently shaded sides. Lighting is diffuse from the upper left.

The source material stays blank; the Echo k is a separate vector layer composed in Icon Composer. Keep the material opaque and full bleed before the platform icon mask is applied.
