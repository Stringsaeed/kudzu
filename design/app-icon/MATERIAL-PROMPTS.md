## Initial material prompt

Use case: sketch-to-render.
Asset type: square macOS/iOS app icon material layer for Icon Composer.
Edit target: the supplied Kudzu keycap icon.
Primary request: turn the illustrated keycap into a convincingly real premium low-profile keyboard key, beautifully restrained Apple hardware studio photography. REMOVE BOTH THE GREEN LEAF AND THE DARK DOT COMPLETELY: the key must be blank, because the exact original colon will be overlaid later as a separate vector printing layer.
Preserve the original upright symmetrical silhouette, almost overhead camera looking very slightly from the front, and full-canvas occupancy. The key is the entire app icon, not a smaller object on a background. Its outer edges touch all four edges of the square image, with transparent corners outside its continuous rounded-square silhouette.
Material: warm paper-white soft-touch PBT plastic, like unglazed porcelain, with extremely fine almost invisible microtexture. Paper-white face around #F6F7F2, softly shaded neutral sides through #E3E6DC to #AEB4A0. Absolutely no green colour accent anywhere on the blank key.
Shape: a wide sculpted top face, very gently concave/dishe​d, a single continuous moulded object. Replace the hard illustrated inner outlines with broad soft rounded transitions and realistic light falloff. The top face sits high with a tiny sliver above; left and right skirts are subtle; the front skirt is visible over approximately the bottom 12 percent. Soft occlusion beneath the rounded lip, softened front corners, a discreet contact-darkening at the very bottom edge. No layered plates, seams or inset rectangular panel.
Lighting: large diffuse softbox above-left and very soft fill on the right, believable continuous shading, broad low-contrast highlights, calm premium product photograph. Depth should come from geometry and soft light, not shiny highlights.
Avoid: text, marks, legend, dots, leaves, stems, symbols, outlines, strokes, dramatic perspective, tilt, floating key, black background, cast shadow outside key, glossy plastic, glass, chrome, inflated toy, harsh bevel bands, noisy texture, extra elements.
Output only the blank keycap with transparent corners, square 1024x1024.
