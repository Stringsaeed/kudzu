# EmptyState

What a pane shows when it has nothing to show: a faint leaf mark, a short title, one sentence that says what to do, and the shortcut.

Consumer provides `title`, children (the sentence, may include a Kbd), optional `icon` (defaults to `leaf`) and `action` (a Button). The mark is `ink-faint`; the title `title`; the body `ink-muted`, at most 280px wide.
